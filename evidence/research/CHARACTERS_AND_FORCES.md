# Characters and forces

This is a compact name-resolution guide for strings, pilot IDs, scenario text,
and save-game investigation. It summarizes the [Sarna character
reference](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/BattleTech:_The_Crescent_Hawks%27_Revenge/Characters)
and the campaign overview.

## Crescent Hawks and Kell Hounds

- **Jason Youngblood** — player character and Crescent Hawks commander.
- **Jeremiah Youngblood** — Jason's missing father and Operation Liberty's
  rescue target.
- **Rex Pearce** — veteran Hawk and Jason's second-in-command; later retired.
- **Kurt Graham** — original Hawk who later forms his own mercenary unit.
- **Rick “Grease” Anderson** — Kell Hound technician who becomes a Hawk pilot.
- **Jen Pearce** — Rex's daughter and 3051 Scout Lance leader.
- **Morgan Kell** — Kell Hounds commander and patron of the Hawks.
- **Caitlin Shaw** — Kell Hounds captain during the Lyons defense.
- **Sergeant Krall** — retrieves Listen-Kill missiles at the Kell Hound base.

## Draconis Combine

- **Chiun** — recurring Kurita opponent on Lyons and Dieron, later Jason's
  commander during the defense of Luthien.
- **Toranaga, Yamashita, Ichi, Hosaka, Higa, Sanjuro, and Takagi** — Kurita
  names visible in the executable pilot-name table.
- **Takashi Kurita** — Coordinator during the Battle of Luthien.

## Other story figures

- **Katrina Steiner** — gives Jason command of the Hawks and later promotes
  him.
- **Melissa Steiner** — recalls the surviving Hawks to Pacifica after Luthien.
- **Gideon Braver Vandenburg** — Blazing Aces commander encountered on Kaesong.
- **Maria Vandenburg** — intended recipient of Gideon's final message.
- **Ilnay Techus** — defeated Clan Galaxy Commander.

## Faction values

The community 150-byte record map reports allegiance at `0x94`, beginning with
`0x00` Crescent Hawks and `0x01` Kell Hounds. Treat the remaining numeric map as
unverified until it is compared systematically with known scenario rosters.
The executable's pilot-name table contains duplicates, so names alone are not
stable character identities.
